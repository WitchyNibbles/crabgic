PROBE A -> c9 ("`spawn`/`resume` refuse to start outside the accepted version range —
               `version-gate.test`")

  Deleting `assertEngineVersionAccepted(this.engineVersionResolver());` from
  `ClaudeEngineAdapter.resume()` reddened EXACTLY ONE test:
      "resume() also refuses synchronously outside the accepted range"
  and that test lives in `packages/engine-claude/src/adapter.test.ts:233`, NOT in
  `version-gate.test.ts`. Every one of `version-gate.test.ts`'s 20 tests stayed GREEN.

  FINDING, and it is a real one. The criterion's SUBSTANCE holds, on both conjuncts:
  `adapter.test.ts:217` pins `spawn()`'s refusal (and additionally proves ZERO sdkQuery calls and
  ZERO journal appends), `adapter.test.ts:233` pins `resume()`'s. Both run in the default gate.
  But the suite the criterion NAMES does not test `spawn` or `resume` at all — it tests the pure
  helper `assertEngineVersionAccepted` plus a baseline-document sync check. A reader following the
  criterion's own pointer would find no spawn/resume assertion there.
  This is a bearer-NAME defect, not a missing guarantee: the box is ticked on the measured
  substance, and the pointer drift is filed as a defect.

PROBE B -> c6 ("`limitSignal` fires against phase-00's captured (or an equivalently
               live-triggered) rate-limit shape — `limit-signal.test`")

  Changing the recorded `utilization` from the captured 0.96 to 0.97 reddened EXACTLY ONE test:

  `limit-signal.test.ts` stayed GREEN.

  FINDING. `limit-signal.test.ts:52-56` asserts `expect(event).toEqual({ type, sessionId,
  ...payload })` — a TAUTOLOGY over whatever the constant happens to hold. The named suite
  therefore pins the normalizer's passthrough, which is real, but it does NOT bind the CAPTURED
  VALUES, so on its own it cannot carry "against phase-00's captured shape".
  What DOES carry that conjunct is `packages/engine-claude/src/event-normalizer.test.ts:90-113`,
  which reads the COMMITTED phase-00 fixtures off disk
  (`event-normalizer.test.ts:18` builds `../../../spikes/fixtures/<name>` URLs) and asserts
  `expect(rateLimitMessages).toHaveLength(16)` with every one normalizing to a `limitSignal`.
  That file also stayed green under this mutation — which is the proof of INDEPENDENCE: the
  fixture-backed suite and the hand-typed constants are two genuinely separate sources, so
  perturbing one does not move the other.
  Secondary finding: `rate-limit-fixtures.test.ts:11-19`'s first case compares
  `RECORDED_RATE_LIMIT_PAYLOADS` against its own four members — the brief's "tautology over a
  literal". Its value-pinning cases (`:29`, `:39`) are the non-vacuous ones, and they are what
