        before final_verifying                               [cli seam e2e]

  and BOTH does-not-halt controls stayed GREEN, which is exactly what makes them
  controls rather than restatements: an unconditional no-op is the world the
  controls describe, so they must survive it.

  M4 — CHANGE THE KIND. `kind: "material_amendment"` -> `kind: "expanded_authority"`.
  The criterion names the kind, so a bearer that merely asserted "some decision
  was journaled" would stay green here — the `toThrow(ConnectorError)` failure
  shape. TWO tests reddened, and they are precisely the two that pin the
  rationale string:

      × a material signal halts an in-flight run: ...         [supervisor unit]
      × a seeded mid-run tracked-field edit, ...              [cli seam e2e]

  Note the M4 count (2) is strictly smaller than M3's (3): the no-resurrect test
  asserts the transition and the decision COUNT but not the kind, so it survives
  a kind swap. That difference is the measurement — the two probes are not
  redundant.

  M1/M2 double as the seam probes. Under M1 (the poll deleted) the cli e2e
  reddens at `second.revisionPoll?.signal?.material`, which proves the e2e
  actually consumes 18's producer rather than a hand-built stand-in inside the
  test file.

MATCHED-STRING HYGIENE, AUDITED AGAINST THE OPPOSITE OUTCOME

  * `"blocked"` is asserted only as the exact value of `RunRecord.runState`, a
    closed 11-member enum field — never as a substring search. The three other
    "blocked" vocabularies this seam touches (17's
    `MilestoneSyncOutcome.status: "blocked"`, `JiraWorkflowStage` "blocked", and
    gate-verdict prose) therefore cannot satisfy it. Every milestone cycle in the
    e2e additionally asserts `outcome.status === "planned"`, so the lint-blocked
    arm cannot stand in unnoticed.
  * `stop condition "material_amendment"` — the controls assert the decision list
    for their runId has LENGTH 0, the strongest form of "does not contain it".
    The non-halted outcome is `{halted:false}` with no `record` and no reason
    string, asserted with an exact `toEqual`, so it is disjoint by SHAPE from the
    halted outcome, not merely by string.
