ASSERTION-STRING HYGIENE

  `MaterialChangeSignal` is a discriminated union: the material arm is
  `{material:true, previousRevision, currentRevision}` and the non-material arm
  is `{material:false}` with no revision members at all. Both are asserted with a
  full-object `toEqual`, so no matched string of the positive case can appear in
  the negative one. Every cycle also asserts `outcome.status === "planned"`, which
  stops the module's OTHER "blocked" vocabulary (`MilestoneSyncOutcome`'s
  `policy_blocked` arm) from silently standing in for a result.
