C. WHAT THESE PROBES DO NOT COVER — stated, not implied
===============================================================================

- ci-run citations' quoted job-log lines are NOT checked by any CI lane. The
  normalization (ANSI strip + timestamp + one-space, with the two-space form
  twelve merged records use grandfathered) is implemented and unit-tested in
  scripts/citation-content/job-log.mjs and driven by --report --job-logs <dir>
  over logs a pass has already downloaded. It needs the network and a token;
  meta-checks has neither. UNVERIFIED against a live job log in this pass.
- Rules 3 (group consecutiveness) and 4 (repeat text) are reported, never
  blocking, and their report output is not mutation-tested beyond the unit
  battery's repeat-text control.
- A cross-file quote written with a bare filename and NO line number resolves
  against the citation's own ref file, because letting a bare filename switch
  files re-pointed seven phase-00 fragments at a deleted path. Such a quote is
  reported ABSENT/MOVED rather than checked against the file it names.
